# Hardware in Bonn, NRW and Germany

Where to get your hands on machines and parts. Closest first: **Bonn** is reachable today,
the rest of **NRW** is a train ride, and the rest of **Germany** ships to your door.

Sections are what you go there *for*. Every entry is tagged with where it is, so you can
scan a section by distance. Opening times, membership terms and prices change — check the
official page before you travel or order.

---

## Build it yourself

*Walk in and use the machines.*

- **[MakerSpace Bonn e.V.](https://www.makerspacebonn.de/)** — An open workshop with around 25 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC mills, a metal shop and equipment you can borrow. — `membership` · `bonn`
- **[Dingfabrik Köln](https://dingfabrik.de/)** — Cologne's FabLab, covering 3D printing, laser cutting, soldering, turning and sewing. — `membership` · `köln`
- **[Stadtbibliothek Köln — 3D printing](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/artikel/08195/index.html)** — The city library lends 3D printers and scanners once you've taken their introduction course. — `library card` · `köln`
- **[Chaosdorf](https://www.chaosdorf.de/)** — Düsseldorf's hackerspace, strongest on electronics and software rather than heavy machines. — `open evenings` · `düsseldorf`
- **[FabLab Aachen](https://fablab-aachen.de/)** — A long-running FabLab with the usual digital fabrication kit. — `membership` · `aachen`
- **[FabLab@FH Dortmund](https://www.fh-dortmund.de/labore/fablab.php)** — A university FabLab that opens to students and private individuals alike. — `open access` · `dortmund`
- **[FabLab Münster](https://www.digitalhub.ms/fablab)** — Prototyping lab and makerspace attached to the city's digital hub. — `membership` · `münster`
- **[Fab Lab Siegen](https://fablab-siegen.de/)** — The open workshop for the south-east corner of the state. — `membership` · `siegen`

## Buy the parts

*Components, boards and kits, ordered online.*

- **[BerryBase](https://www.berrybase.de/)** — Single-board computers, sensors and maker kits, with the best selection for Raspberry Pi work. — `€` · `hamburg`
- **[reichelt elektronik](https://www.reichelt.com/)** — A huge general electronics catalogue, the default for components by the bagful. — `€` · `niedersachsen`
- **[Pollin](https://www.pollin.de/)** — Cheap parts, surplus stock and kits, worth checking before paying full price. — `€` · `bayern`
- **[Eckstein](https://eckstein-shop.de/)** — Modules, breakouts and development boards, often carrying things others don't. — `€` · `germany`
- **[RS](https://de.rs-online.com/)** — Professional-grade components with reliable datasheets and next-day delivery. — `€€` · `germany`
- **[Mouser](https://www.mouser.de/)** — Deep catalogue of specific chips, for when a part number has to match exactly. — `€€` · `germany`

## Get a circuit board made

*Send a design, get boards back.*

- **[Aisler](https://aisler.net/)** — European board house built for small prototype runs, with straightforward pricing. — `€€` · `eu`
- **[Eurocircuits](https://www.eurocircuits.com/)** — Belgian manufacturer offering prototypes and small series with transparent online quotes. — `€€` · `eu`
- **[WEdirekt](https://www.wedirekt.de/)** — German board house with fast turnaround on prototype orders. — `€€` · `germany`
- **[JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/)** — By far the cheapest option, at the cost of longer shipping and customs handling. — `€` · `china`

## Find another workshop

*Spaces open and close faster than any list survives. These directories stay current.*

- **[Verbund Offener Werkstätten — workshop search](https://offene-werkstaetten.org/de/werkstatt-suche)** — Searchable map of open workshops across Germany, filterable by location. — `free` · `germany`
- **[fablabs.io — labs in Germany](https://www.fablabs.io/labs?q%5Bcountry_code_eq%5D=DE)** — The global FabLab network's own directory, filtered to Germany. — `free` · `germany`
- **[Fab:UNIverse](https://fabuniverse.github.io/website/labs/)** — Lists makerspaces run by universities in German-speaking countries. — `free` · `universities`
