# Bonn for builders

The city itself as a place to build: what it is good at, what a month costs, and what an arriving
student has to do first. A playbook for Bonn alone, on the model of the city playbooks at
[justmovetoeurope.com](https://justmovetoeurope.com/).

Nothing here is legal or immigration advice. Figures checked 2026-09; rents, fees and
administrative rules change, and the official pages linked below govern.

## What Bonn is good for

*The case for the city, made honestly and in one paragraph.*

Bonn carries more robotics and AI research than a city of its size would suggest. The University of
Bonn runs the PhenoRob Cluster of Excellence in agricultural robotics — the German research cluster
sitting most directly on Robonn's own domain — alongside groups in photogrammetry, autonomous
systems and machine learning, and hosts one of the sites of the Lamarr Institute, a national AI
centre on permanent federal and state funding. Fraunhofer IAIS is a short train ride away in Sankt
Augustin, and the DLR site at Köln-Porz is closer to Bonn than to central Köln. Two DAX-listed
companies are headquartered in the city, Deutsche Telekom and DHL Group, the second of which puts
logistics automation on the doorstep. The federal cybersecurity agency and around twenty United
Nations institutions are here as well. Köln and Düsseldorf sit within an hour on a ticket most
students already hold.

## What Bonn is not

*The tradeoffs, stated plainly, because the omission would be noticed.*

The startup density is elsewhere. Consumer and software founders concentrate in Köln, Düsseldorf
and Berlin, and Bonn's weight sits in research institutes and large corporates rather than in young
companies. No venue in the city sells space for driving, flying or testing a robot; the Space
section documents that gap rather than solving it. Hall-of-residence places are allocated months
ahead and the private market has tightened. Municipal and immigration business is conducted largely
in German whatever the English landing pages suggest, and appointment availability binds harder
than paperwork does. The city is also quiet by the standards of the cities it is compared against:
much of it empties at the weekend, and the evening scene belongs to Köln.

## Somewhere to live

*The scarce resource, and the one to start on before arriving.*

- **[Studierendenwerk Bonn — accommodation](https://www.studierendenwerk-bonn.de/en/accommodation)** — Hall-of-residence places whose rent includes utilities and internet, allocated on application several months ahead of the semester. — `€` · `bonn` · `housing`
- **[H-BRS accommodation for international students](https://www.h-brs.de/en/accommodation-international-students)** — The equivalent route for anyone enrolled at the university of applied sciences in Sankt Augustin, a short train ride from Bonn. — `€` · `bonn` · `housing`
- **[WG-Gesucht](https://www.wg-gesucht.de/en/)** — The shared-flat market where most students who miss a hall place eventually find a room, and the reason a Bonn search should begin months early. — `€€` · `germany` · `housing`
- **[Living in Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/university/university-life/living-in-bonn)** — The university's orientation on districts, transport and daily life, useful for deciding where in the city to look. — `free` · `bonn`

## What a month costs

*Ranges only, and the figure that actually varies is rent.*

The university puts average monthly costs in Bonn at roughly €900–1,100, of which rent is both the
largest line and the one a student can influence. A hall-of-residence room runs from about €250,
while a room in a shared flat is commonly €450–600. Health cover for students adds roughly €100 a
month. On top of that sits the semester contribution of €345–363, paid once per semester rather
than monthly, more than half of which buys the Deutschland-Semesterticket outright.

- **[Cost of studying at Uni Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/studying/application-admission-and-enrollment/costs)** — The university's own breakdown of the semester contribution and the monthly living costs it expects a student to budget for. — `free` · `bonn` · `official`
- **[Studierendenwerk Bonn](https://www.studierendenwerk-bonn.de/en/)** — The body that administers the semester contribution, the refectories and the halls of residence, and states what the contribution covers. — `free` · `bonn` · `official`

## Getting around

*Bonn is small enough to cycle; the ticket matters for everywhere else.*

- **[Deutschlandticket](https://www.deutschlandticket.de/)** — A flat monthly fare covering regional and local public transport nationwide, reducing the cost of reaching Köln, Düsseldorf and Aachen to nothing extra. — `€` · `germany` · `transit`
- **[VRS](https://www.vrs.de/)** — The regional transport network Bonn belongs to, and the source for the timetables and zones the Deutschlandticket is used on. — `free` · `nrw` · `transit`

Most enrolled students already hold the Deutschland-Semesterticket through the semester
contribution, which makes travel across NRW a sunk cost rather than a decision.

## The research around you

*The reason to be in Bonn rather than in a cheaper city.*

- **[PhenoRob](https://www.phenorob.de/)** — A Cluster of Excellence applying robotics and machine learning to crop farming, and the group whose subject matter overlaps Robonn's agriculture domain most directly. — `free` · `bonn` · `research`
- **[Lamarr Institute](https://lamarr-institute.org/)** — One of Germany's national AI centres, with a site at the University of Bonn and permanent federal and state funding. — `free` · `bonn` · `research`
- **[Photogrammetry and Robotics](https://www.ipb.uni-bonn.de/)** — The university group working on SLAM, mapping and perception for field robots, whose published code and datasets are usable directly. — `free` · `bonn` · `research`
- **[Autonomous Intelligent Systems](https://www.ais.uni-bonn.de/)** — The university group behind Bonn's humanoid and mobile manipulation work, and a regular entrant in international robotics competitions. — `free` · `bonn` · `research`
- **[Institute of Computer Science](https://www.informatik.uni-bonn.de/en)** — The department these groups sit in, and the route to lecture courses, theses and open positions in robotics and AI. — `free` · `bonn` · `research`
- **[Fraunhofer IAIS](https://www.iais.fraunhofer.de/en.html)** — An applied research institute in machine learning and big data at Sankt Augustin, reachable from Bonn in well under an hour. — `free` · `nrw` · `research`
- **[DLR](https://www.dlr.de/en)** — The German aerospace centre, whose Köln-Porz site carries robotics and automation work and sits closer to Bonn than to central Köln. — `free` · `nrw` · `research`

## Money and advice

*Bonn is better placed for this than its size suggests, and it is covered in full elsewhere.*

Germany's most active early-stage investor was founded in Bonn and is still headquartered here, and
the university runs its own transfer centre offering free advice to students well before anything
is fundable. The state stipend for NRW founders and the federal EXIST programme are both open to
student teams, and the chamber of commerce acts as the regional contact point for the former.

None of that is restated here. The contacts, terms and the order to approach them in are in
[nrw_investor_contacts.md](nrw_investor_contacts.md).
