# Space in Bonn

Space to meet, space to build, and space to run a robot on the floor. Opening hours and booking
rules change; official pages should be checked before travelling.

---

## Somewhere to meet

*Free for students, and sufficient for most working sessions.*

- **[ULB Bonn group rooms](https://www.ulb.uni-bonn.de/en/using-the-library/studying-and-working/main-library)** — Bookable group work rooms with screens, open to any holder of a library card. — `free`
- **[H-BRS library group rooms](https://www.h-brs.de/de/bib/gruppenarbeit-der-bibliothek)** — Open seating and lockable group rooms at the university of applied sciences in Sankt Augustin. — `free`
- **[Stadtbibliothek Bonn](https://lib.bonn.de/)** — The city library's central branch in the Haus der Bildung, open to everyone rather than only students. — `free`

---

## Rooms through the university

*The route most student groups overlook, and the one that opens the most doors.*

- **[AStA — student groups](https://asta-bonn.de/de/service/studentische-gruppen)** — Registration as a student group permits requests for university rooms, applications for funding, and advertising across campus.
- **[Referat für Kultur und studentische Initiativen](https://asta-bonn.de/de/referate/referat-fuer-kultur-und-studentische-initiativen)** — The AStA department that handles student initiatives and the contact point for questions about registration.

## Somewhere to work on hardware

- **[MakerSpace Bonn](https://www.makerspacebonn.de/)** — An open workshop with open floor area, work benches, and equipment available on loan, on a membership basis. *(Its machines are listed in [nrw_hardware_contacts.md](nrw_hardware_contacts.md).)*

---

## Desks and meeting rooms

- **[DIGITALHUB.DE](https://www.digitalhub.de/en/coworking)** — Two Bonn locations with 46 coworking desks and five meeting and seminar rooms, accessible around the clock. *(Its accelerator programmes are in [nrw_investor_contacts.md](nrw_investor_contacts.md).)*

## Somewhere to test a robot

*Nothing in Bonn advertises this, so it has to be improvised.*

No venue in the city offers space specifically for driving, flying or testing a robot. Until that
changes, the practical alternatives are:

- A **seminar room booked through the AStA** — sufficient floor area for a mobile base, and free to a
  registered student group.
- The **MakerSpace Bonn floor** — a working environment where machine and robot testing is expected.
- A **quiet campus courtyard or corridor** outside teaching hours, for anything requiring more
  distance than a room allows.

Securing a dedicated workshop space is a Year 2 goal on the club roadmap. Until then this section
documents a gap rather than a set of options.
