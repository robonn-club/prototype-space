## Make {#make}

The city's open workshop, and two less obvious routes.

- [[MakerSpace Bonn e.V.]] — 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC mills and a metal shop, on a membership basis. Go to one of its free open evenings ("Offene Tür") first; every machine needs an induction before you use it alone.
- [[HRZ 3D printing service]]
- [[Datenburg e.V.]]

A train ride away on the semester ticket: [[Dingfabrik Köln]] and
[[Stadtbibliothek Köln — 3D printing|the Cologne city library's 3D printers]], the
[[FabLab Aachen]] and [[FabLab@FH Dortmund]]. [All workshops in NRW](../docs/nrw_hardware_contacts.md#open-workshops).

## Test {#test}

No venue in Bonn rents out space for driving, flying or testing robots. What works in practice:

- **A seminar room** booked through the [[AStA — student groups|AStA]]. Registered student
  groups can book lecture halls and rooms, with floor enough for a mobile base.
- **The [[MakerSpace Bonn e.V.|MakerSpace]] floor**, where running machines is expected.
- **Campus corridors and courtyards** outside teaching hours. Ask the building's caretaker
  first.
- **Real crop rows** at [[Campus Klein-Altendorf]], the university's outdoor laboratory,
  reached through the research groups that work there.
- **Drones**: indoors first. Outdoors, most of the city is covered by geographical zones; see
  [the drone rules](tracks/drone.md#before-you-fly).

A dedicated workshop space is a Year 2 goal on Robonn's roadmap. Until then, this is a
gap, and it is written down as one.

## Money {#money}

Money for parts is the hardest thing to find at the first-build stage. What exists:

- **Registered student groups.** Through the [[AStA — student groups|AStA]], a registered
  student group can book rooms, borrow the AStA's equipment, and apply for money for events that
  benefit Bonn's students. The money is reimbursed afterwards against receipts; it is not a
  budget for parts.
- **Your course.** Builds that are part of a course or thesis can use university services, such
  as the free [[HRZ 3D printing service]], through teaching staff.
- **Once it could become a company.** Free advice from [[enaCom — Uni Bonn Transfer Center]], then
  grants such as [[Gründungsstipendium.NRW]] and [[EXIST-Gründungsstipendium]]. See
  [after the first build](steps/after.md#start-a-company).

Until then, a first build is paid for by choosing a cheap track. The
[sensor rig](tracks/sensor.md) and [software](tracks/software.md) tracks cost little or
nothing.
