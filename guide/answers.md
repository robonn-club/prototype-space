## Where can I 3D print, laser cut or solder?

[[MakerSpace Bonn e.V.|MakerSpace Bonn]] has all of it, on a membership basis. Go to a free open
evening first; every machine needs an induction.

[Places to make in Bonn](bonn.md#make)

## Where do I buy parts?

Online: [[BerryBase]] and [[reichelt elektronik]] for most things, [[Mouser]] when a part number
must match. Shops in Germany usually deliver within a few working days.

[Choosing and ordering parts](steps/parts.md)

## Where can I test a robot?

Nowhere in Bonn rents out space for it. Book a seminar room through the AStA, use the MakerSpace
floor, or ask for a corridor outside teaching hours.

[Where to test in Bonn](steps/test.md#where-to-test-in-bonn)

## Who can help?

The [[Robonn]] Discord, [[Datenburg e.V.|Datenburg]]'s open evening every Tuesday, and at H-BRS
the [[b-it-bots]] RoboCup team, which is open to students at any level.

[People in Bonn](bonn.md#people)

## What will it cost?

Nothing until step 3. After that, from under €250 for a sensor rig to €1,000 and more for a
field or walking robot.

[Compare the tracks](tracks/index.md)

## Is there money for parts?

Rarely, at this stage. Registered student groups get rooms and event funding through the AStA,
and startup grants come later.

[Money in Bonn](bonn.md#money)

## Can I fly a drone in Bonn?

Outdoors only with insurance, after registering with the LBA if it weighs over 249 g or has a
camera, and outside the city's many no-fly zones. Start indoors.

[The drone rules](tracks/drone.md#before-you-fly)

## When should it be done?

Pick a date: Maker Faire Ruhr in Dortmund on 13–14 March 2027, or the Nacht der Technik in Bonn
on 1 October 2027.

[Every date](steps/show.md#pick-a-date)
