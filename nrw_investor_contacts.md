# Startup funding in Bonn and NRW

Who to talk to when a prototype turns out to be worth more than a prototype. Sections are
what you go to them *for*; every entry is tagged with what it gives you and where it is.

Bonn is a better place for this than it looks — Germany's most active early-stage investor
was founded here, and the university runs its own transfer centre.

Programme terms, deadlines and eligibility change constantly. Check the official page before
relying on anything here.

---

## Get advice first

*Free, close, and the fastest way to find out if your idea is fundable.*

- **[enaCom — Uni Bonn Transfer Center](https://www.uni-bonn.de/de/forschung-lehre/transfer/transfercenter-enacom)** — The university's own startup and transfer office, free to students and the closest door to knock on. — `free advice` · `bonn`
- **[enaCom — funding opportunities](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/research-and-teaching/transfer-center-enacom/funding-opportunities/funding-opportunities)** — Their maintained overview of the funding programmes a Uni Bonn founder can actually apply to. — `overview` · `bonn`
- **[IHK Bonn/Rhein-Sieg](https://www.ihk-bonn.de/unternehmensfoerderung/existenzgruendung/gruenderstipendiumnrw)** — The chamber of commerce advises on founding and acts as a regional contact point for the NRW stipend. — `free advice` · `bonn`
- **[Campus Start-ups NRW](https://www.campus-start-ups.nrw/)** — Statewide overview of what every NRW university offers its student founders. — `overview` · `nrw`

## Money without giving up equity

*Grants and stipends. Realistically the first money a student founder gets.*

- **[Gründungsstipendium.NRW](https://www.gründungsstipendium.nrw/)** — €1,200 a month for up to a year for teams of up to three, if you live in NRW. — `grant` · `nrw`
- **[Gründungsstipendium.NRW — NRW.BANK page](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/15916/gruendungsstipendiumnrw.html)** — The formal terms and application route for the same stipend. — `grant` · `nrw`
- **[EXIST-Gründungsstipendium](https://www.exist.de/)** — The federal stipend for startups coming out of universities and research institutions. — `grant` · `germany`
- **[EXIST via NRW.BANK](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/15118/exist-gruendungsstipendium.html)** — The NRW-facing summary of EXIST, useful for checking fit before applying. — `grant` · `nrw`

## Join a programme

*Space, mentoring and a network rather than cash.*

- **[DIGITALHUB.DE](https://www.digitalhub.de/en)** — Bonn's startup hub, running accelerator programmes and connecting founders to investors and partners. — `accelerator` · `bonn`
- **[STARTPLATZ](https://www.startplatz.de/en/)** — The largest incubator and accelerator in NRW, with coworking in both Cologne and Düsseldorf. — `incubator` · `köln`
- **[Gateway Exzellenz Start-up Center](https://gateway-unikoeln.de/en)** — University of Cologne's start-up centre, working with RWTH Aachen and HHU Düsseldorf to scale ventures. — `accelerator` · `köln`
- **[DWNRW hub network](https://www.wirtschaft.nrw/dwnrw-hubs)** — The state's digital hubs across Aachen, Bonn, Düsseldorf, Münster, Bielefeld and the Ruhr. — `network` · `nrw`

## Raise from investors

*The people who actually write cheques. Expect to give up equity.*

- **[High-Tech Gründerfonds](https://www.htgf.de/en/)** — Germany's most active early-stage investor, headquartered in Bonn, investing pre-seed and seed in robotics and deep tech. — `equity` · `bonn`
- **[HTGF via NRW.BANK](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/15134/high-tech-gruenderfonds-htgf.html)** — The funding-product view of HTGF, with the terms stated plainly. — `equity` · `nrw`
- **[NRW.BANK](https://www.nrwbank.de/)** — The state development bank, with equity and loan products for young companies alongside its grant programmes. — `equity + loans` · `nrw`
- **[DeepTech & Climate Fonds](https://www.dtcf.de/)** — Federal fund for deep-tech companies past seed that need serious growth capital. — `equity` · `germany`
